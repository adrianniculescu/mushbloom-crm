# Reposition Mushbloom around practical AI automation

## Goal
Reframe Mushbloom as the international no-code AI automation agency for SMEs and owner-run businesses, while keeping the existing pages, specialist services and visual identity intact.

## Homepage
- Replace the “AI workforce” hero with: **Turn messy workflows into simple AI systems that book more jobs and save hours**.
- Use the supplied SME-focused subheadline and make **Book a free 20-minute audit** the primary action.
- Replace the current results panel and unsupported headline statistics with practical workflow examples: missed enquiries, quote follow-up, booking and repetitive admin.
- Rework the existing homepage sections, without changing the overall page flow, to cover:
  - the problems owner-run businesses recognise;
  - three plain-English outcomes: capture more leads, follow up consistently and save admin time;
  - the four-step delivery process from discovery to proving value;
  - target customer groups;
  - why Mushbloom: no jargon, real workflows, quick implementation, international delivery and flexible engagement;
  - the low-risk audit, agency experience since 2014 and final audit call-to-action.
- Keep specialist services such as LLMboost, Newswire, video, development and lead data visible as supporting capabilities rather than the core positioning.

## Site-wide positioning
- Update navigation wording and calls-to-action from generic “Book a Call” to the free audit.
- Update the shared enquiry section to focus on the customer’s biggest workflow bottleneck and remove the old autonomous-workforce and 37% guarantee language.
- Update the footer description and relevant About/Agency copy so the same SME, no-code and outcome-first story is consistent.
- Preserve existing contact details, forms, service pages, Wiki content, partner links and legal pages.

## Search and AI visibility
- Set the homepage title and description from the brief: **AI Automation for SMEs & Owner-Run Businesses | Mushbloom** and its supplied description.
- Update the homepage’s organisation/service structured data to describe global no-code AI automation for SMEs, with founding/operating history aligned to “since 2014”.
- Update `llms.txt` core entity, services and FAQ wording to the new positioning while retaining all specialist URLs and factual service details.
- Remove old homepage-level claims that conflict with the new narrative, while leaving service-specific claims on their dedicated pages unchanged.

## Validation
- Check desktop and mobile layouts, audit links, section navigation, metadata and structured data.
- Confirm the enquiry form still submits and the current preview builds without errors.
