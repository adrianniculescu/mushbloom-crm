# What is Embedding?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-embedding.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

An embedding is a numerical representation of content used to compare patterns of meaning. Retrieval systems can use embeddings to find relevant passages even when wording differs; similarity does not establish factual correctness.

## Analogy & mental model

It is a map that places related ideas near each other, not a stamp saying they are true.

Use retrieval tests and source checks alongside semantic matching.

## Why it matters

Different wording can describe the same business issue. Embeddings help systems retrieve passages by semantic similarity, improving recall when exact keywords differ. They can also retrieve plausible but unsuitable material, so quality and access controls remain essential.

## Practical business example

A query about “unanswered sales requests” retrieves a document headed “missed enquiries”, even though the exact phrase differs.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Convert documents and queries into numerical representations with a compatible model.

2. Compare representations to retrieve nearby candidate passages.

3. Filter and assess candidates before using them as answer evidence.

## Core components

- Embedding model

- Vector representation

- Similarity comparison

High similarity does not establish truth, freshness or permission to disclose a document.

## Embedding vs Keyword search

Keyword search matches terms directly. Embedding search matches learned patterns of meaning; hybrid retrieval can use both rather than choosing one exclusively.
