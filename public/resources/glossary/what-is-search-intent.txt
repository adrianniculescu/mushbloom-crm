# What is Search intent?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-search-intent.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

Search intent is the task or need behind a query, such as learning a concept, comparing suppliers or booking a service. Useful pages match that need rather than only repeating the query’s keywords.

## Analogy & mental model

Someone asking for a plumber needs different information from someone asking how a tap works.

Separate educational resources from service pages and give each a relevant next step.

## Why it matters

A page can attract visitors without attracting customers if it answers the wrong stage of their journey. Intent clarifies whether a reader needs a definition, a comparison or a purchasing decision. Matching that need improves the usefulness of the page and its next step.

## Practical business example

A glossary explains what an agent is, while a service page explains scope, approval rules and how to request an assessment.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Collect the query and the likely task behind it.

2. Choose a page format that resolves that task with appropriate depth.

3. Offer a next step that follows naturally from the answer rather than interrupting it.

## Core components

- Reader task

- Appropriate format

- Relevant continuation

Do not assume every informational search is a purchase-ready lead.

## Search intent vs Keyword

A keyword is the wording of a search. Intent is the underlying need, which may be shared by many different phrases.
