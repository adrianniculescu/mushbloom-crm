What is Workflow automation?

Plain-text edition

Definition & overview

Workflow automation connects steps in a business process so defined events trigger actions with less manual effort. AI may handle language or judgement within the workflow, while deterministic rules control the surrounding process.

Analogy & mental model

A relay team passes the right information to the next person without repeatedly retyping it.

Map the existing process, define exceptions and automate one measurable bottleneck first.

Why Workflow automation matters

Repetitive handoffs often cause delays, missing information and duplicated admin. Automation can make those handoffs more consistent, but a broken process becomes a faster broken process if nobody maps it first. Start with the bottleneck and its exceptions, then choose tools.

Practical business example

A completed enquiry form creates a record, alerts the responsible person and schedules a reminder if no follow-up is logged. AI may draft a response without independently approving a quote.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How Workflow automation works

  1. Map the trigger, information inputs and required business outcome.
  2. Connect defined actions and use AI only where interpretation or language adds value.
  3. Track completion, route exceptions and improve the process using actual operational feedback.

Core components & mechanisms

  • Trigger and state
  • Actions and branching
  • Exceptions and ownership

Avoid automating unclear ownership or sensitive actions without a review path.

Workflow automation vs AI agent

Workflow automation can be entirely deterministic. An agent may choose actions within a workflow, but the surrounding process still defines allowed transitions and approvals.
