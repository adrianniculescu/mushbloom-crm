# What is IndexNow?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-indexnow.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

IndexNow is a protocol for notifying participating search engines when website URLs are added, updated or deleted. A notification helps discovery but does not guarantee indexing, and participation is engine-specific.

## Analogy & mental model

It tells the librarian that a book changed; the librarian still decides whether and when to catalogue it.

Notify genuine canonical URL changes through a verified implementation, not repeated unchanged submissions.

## Why it matters

Search engines need to learn when content changes. IndexNow can reduce reliance on waiting for rediscovery by notifying participating engines. It is a change signal, not a shortcut around quality, canonical selection or indexing eligibility.

## Practical business example

A published guide is updated with a corrected technical explanation and the site sends its canonical URL as a changed resource.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Establish the required key verification for the host.

2. Send valid public URLs that were actually added, updated or deleted.

3. Handle response errors and inspect downstream indexing through the engine’s tools.

## Core components

- Host verification

- Changed URL notification

- Participating engine

Support is engine-specific. Do not assume a notification is a direct Google submission or an AI citation request.

## IndexNow vs XML sitemap

A sitemap lists preferred URLs for discovery. IndexNow notifies participating engines about specific changes; neither confirms that the notified content is indexed.
