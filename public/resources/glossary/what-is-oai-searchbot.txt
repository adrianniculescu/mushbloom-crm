# What is OAI-SearchBot?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-oai-searchbot.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

OAI-SearchBot is OpenAI’s crawler for its search features. It has a different purpose from GPTBot, which relates to model training; crawler access does not guarantee a citation.

## Analogy & mental model

One researcher gathers references for answers; another gathers material for training.

Review search and training crawler preferences separately and check that your hosting does not block intended access.

## Why it matters

Search crawling and model-training crawling have different purposes. Businesses should make intentional choices about each instead of treating all AI traffic as identical. Access also depends on hosting and security controls, not only the presence of a robots.txt rule.

## Practical business example

A publisher allows search discovery while reviewing training access separately, then checks that its firewall does not block legitimate search requests.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Review OpenAI’s current documentation for the crawler’s purpose and verification information.

2. Set the intended search crawler preferences in robots.txt.

3. Check actual fetch behaviour and hosting restrictions for public source pages.

## Core components

- Crawler identity

- Search access preference

- Hosting response

Allowing a crawler is permission to access, not a promise of inclusion or citation.

## OAI-SearchBot vs GPTBot

GPTBot is associated with model training. OAI-SearchBot supports search features; choosing a rule for one does not automatically express the same preference for the other.
