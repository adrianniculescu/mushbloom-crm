# What is Agentic commerce?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-agentic-commerce.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

Agentic commerce describes buying journeys in which AI agents help discover products, compare options or carry out authorised purchasing tasks. The degree of automation varies; payment, identity and consent still need explicit controls.

## Analogy & mental model

A shopping assistant can shortlist options, but must operate within the buyer’s agreed budget and authority.

Keep prices, availability and returns accurate, and make approval requirements explicit.

## Why it matters

AI assistance can change how customers compare and purchase products, but merchants still need trustworthy operational data. Accurate prices, availability and policies matter more than promotional wording. Businesses should distinguish recommendation-only experiences from workflows that can actually create orders or payments.

## Practical business example

An assistant shortlists compatible office supplies and prepares an order for the buyer to approve, rather than committing a company to unreviewed purchases.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. The buyer defines a need and may specify constraints such as budget or delivery requirements.

2. An assistant finds and compares supported options using available product data.

3. Any purchase proceeds through an authorised merchant and payment process, with explicit controls on commitment.

## Core components

- Product discovery

- Comparison and buyer constraints

- Authorised purchasing

Do not assume all assistants, merchants or payment providers support the same capabilities.

## Agentic commerce vs ACP

Agentic commerce is the broad practice of agent-assisted buying. ACP is one specific checkout protocol; it does not cover every discovery channel, commercial negotiation or procurement process.
