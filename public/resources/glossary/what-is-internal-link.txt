# What is Internal link?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-internal-link.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

An internal link connects one page on a website to another page on the same website. Descriptive, crawlable links help readers navigate and search engines discover relationships between pages.

## Analogy & mental model

It is a signpost from one aisle of a shop to the next relevant aisle.

Link definitions to detailed guides and guides to the relevant service when the connection is useful.

## Why it matters

A good internal link helps a visitor take the next relevant step and gives crawlers a route to additional pages. It should explain the relationship between content, not merely increase link volume. Broken or vague links interrupt both navigation and discovery.

## Practical business example

A short RAG definition links to the full implementation guide, which in turn points to the supervised-agent service where appropriate.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Choose a related destination that genuinely extends the current subject.

2. Use a normal crawlable link with descriptive anchor text.

3. Check the destination and keep the relationship relevant as content changes.

## Core components

- Source page

- Descriptive anchor

- Valid destination

Avoid stuffing every paragraph with links or linking to pages that do not answer the implied question.

## Internal link vs Sitemap

An internal link supplies context within the reading experience. A sitemap lists URLs for discovery but does not explain every page relationship to visitors.
