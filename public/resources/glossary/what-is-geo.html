Definition & overview

Generative engine optimisation is an industry term for improving the discoverability and usefulness of content in AI-generated responses. It overlaps with SEO and AEO; it is not a separate guaranteed ranking system.

Analogy & mental model

GEO prepares your research to be useful when an assistant writes an answer from several sources.

Publish clear definitions, original evidence and accessible supporting pages, then measure observed outcomes.

Why GEO matters

Generated answers may combine several sources, so a useful page must contribute something clear and defensible. Original explanations, data and comparisons can provide that contribution. GEO work should be tied to observed discovery and business outcomes rather than promises to control a model’s recommendations.

Practical business example

An agency publishes a comparison of supervised agent workflows and fixed automations, explaining where each is appropriate rather than claiming one is always superior.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How GEO works

  1. Map the questions for which your expertise is genuinely relevant.
  2. Create accessible, evidence-backed content with clear definitions and relationships.
  3. Observe citations and referrals across a documented sample, then improve gaps.

Core components & mechanisms

  • Useful source material
  • Technical discoverability
  • Repeatable measurement

Industry usage varies and no content pattern forces an engine to recommend a business.

GEO vs SEO

SEO supports discovery in search results. GEO focuses on usefulness in generated responses; both depend on accessible content, relevance and credibility.
