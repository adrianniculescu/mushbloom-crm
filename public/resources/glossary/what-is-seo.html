What is SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)?

Definition & overview

Search engine optimisation is the practice of helping search engines discover, understand and present useful website content to relevant users. It includes technical access, content quality and a usable website.

Analogy & mental model

You make a shop easy to find, label what it sells and give visitors a reason to stay.

Fix access and indexing first, then improve pages around real customer questions and conversions.

Why SEO matters

Organic visibility begins with accessible, useful pages that match a real need. Traffic alone does not solve a business problem if readers cannot find the relevant offer or complete an enquiry. SEO should therefore connect technical evidence with the commercial journey.

Practical business example

A service business resolves indexing issues, publishes answers to common buyer questions and tracks successfully submitted enquiries rather than only page views.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How SEO works

  1. Check that important pages can be discovered, fetched and considered for indexing.
  2. Improve useful content, internal connections and the website’s clarity.
  3. Measure relevant search queries, landing-page behaviour and successful lead actions.

Core components & mechanisms

  • Technical access
  • Useful content
  • Performance measurement

No agency can guarantee a search engine’s ranking decisions or a fixed number of organic leads.

SEO vs Paid search

SEO improves organic discoverability. Paid search buys placements through an advertising system; neither approach guarantees that a visitor becomes a customer.
