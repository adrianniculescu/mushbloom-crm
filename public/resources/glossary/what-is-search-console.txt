# What is Google Search Console?

Source: https://mushbloom.uk/resources/glossary/what-is-search-console.html

By Mushbloom. Published and updated: 2026-10-12.

## Definition & overview

Google Search Console is Google’s service for verified website owners to inspect search performance, indexing and technical reports. It supplies evidence about Google Search, not a complete view of all AI engines or every lead.

## Analogy & mental model

It is a diagnostic panel showing how one search service sees your site.

Verify ownership, submit the sitemap and inspect important pages before guessing about organic visibility.

## Why it matters

Visibility problems need evidence about discovery and indexing. Search Console can show how Google sees a verified site, while analytics shows what visitors do after arriving. Together they support diagnosis, but neither establishes performance in every AI product.

## Practical business example

An owner discovers that a guide is indexed but receives few impressions for buyer questions, so the next task differs from fixing a page that was never indexed.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

## How it works

1. Verify ownership of the intended site property.

2. Submit the sitemap and inspect representative public URLs.

3. Review queries, impressions, clicks and indexing reports to prioritise actual problems.

## Core components

- Verified property

- URL inspection

- Search and indexing reports

Report availability can lag, and verifying one property does not automatically verify every domain variant.

## Google Search Console vs Google Analytics

Search Console reports Google search and indexing information. Analytics reports measured on-site activity and attribution; their scopes and counting methods differ.
