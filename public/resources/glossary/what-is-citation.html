What is Citation in an AI answer?

Plain-text edition

Definition & overview

An AI citation is a reference or link attached to a generated answer that attributes information to a source. A brand mention without a source link is not the same as a citation, and a citation does not prove the answer is accurate.

Analogy & mental model

It is a footnote in a report, not merely your company name appearing in a sentence.

Record the answer, query, engine, date and actual linked page when checking citations.

Why Citation in an AI answer matters

A linked source gives readers a way to verify and explore an AI answer. For a business, relevance matters as much as frequency: a citation for a buyer’s implementation question is different from a passing historical reference. Links also need to support the actual claim being made.

Practical business example

An answer about supervised AI links to a page explaining approval rules. The source is useful because the linked passage actually describes those rules.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How Citation in an AI answer works

  1. Run a documented question in a specified engine and capture the answer.
  2. Inspect the source link and confirm that it points to your page.
  3. Check relevance, factual accuracy and whether a visit or enquiry follows.

Core components & mechanisms

  • Answer and query
  • Linked source URL
  • Claim-to-source relationship

A source link can be misplaced or fail to support the generated claim.

Citation in an AI answer vs Brand mention

A mention names a company. A citation attributes information to a source, usually through a link; the two require separate measurements.
