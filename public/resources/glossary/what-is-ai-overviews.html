What is AI Overviews?

Definition & overview

AI Overviews are Google Search features that produce AI-generated summaries for some searches and may include links to supporting web pages. Availability and source selection depend on the query and Google’s systems.

Analogy & mental model

Instead of only a shelf of books, the librarian gives a summary and points to selected books.

Maintain normal SEO eligibility and useful text; there is no special markup that guarantees inclusion.

Why AI Overviews matters

A summary can answer part of a search before the user reaches a website. That changes where discovery happens, but a cited page still needs to offer deeper value. Businesses should treat summary visibility as one observation, not proof that qualified traffic or revenue increased.

Practical business example

A software buyer sees an overview of integration options and follows a source to inspect detailed limitations and implementation advice.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How AI Overviews works

  1. Google assesses a query and whether an AI summary is appropriate.
  2. Its systems retrieve relevant information and generate a response with selected links.
  3. Users may inspect those links or continue searching; source inclusion can vary between runs.

Core components & mechanisms

  • Query eligibility
  • Retrieved web evidence
  • Summary and supporting links

Google does not require a special AI schema or an llms.txt file for these features.

AI Overviews vs Featured snippet

A featured snippet extracts content from a selected page. An AI Overview generates a synthesis that may rely on several sources and is not the same search feature.
