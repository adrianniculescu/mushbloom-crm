What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)?

Definition & overview

Answer engine optimisation is the practice of making information easier for answer systems to find, understand and use when responding to a question. It covers clear answers, reliable evidence and accessible pages, not a guaranteed placement.

Analogy & mental model

AEO is like giving a helpful librarian a precise answer with the right reference attached.

Answer a real buyer question directly, then explain the evidence, limits and next step.

Why AEO matters

Buyers increasingly ask complete questions rather than browse many supplier pages. A precise answer can help them understand your expertise before they contact you. The commercial value depends on answering a question that matters to your customers, not simply earning an isolated snippet.

Practical business example

A bookkeeping business explains which documents a new client needs, then links to its onboarding service. The answer resolves uncertainty before the visitor makes contact.

Illustrative scenario, not a client case study or promised result.

How AEO works

  1. Identify recurring questions from enquiries and sales calls, including objections and implementation concerns.
  2. Write a direct answer first, then add the evidence, conditions and a concrete example.
  3. Check that the page is accessible and measure whether readers continue to a relevant guide or service.

Core components & mechanisms

  • A specific reader question
  • A self-contained answer with evidence
  • A relevant next step

Answer formatting alone cannot make inaccurate or unoriginal content authoritative.

AEO vs GEO

AEO emphasises answering questions; GEO emphasises usefulness in generated answers that may combine several sources. They overlap with ordinary SEO rather than replacing it.
